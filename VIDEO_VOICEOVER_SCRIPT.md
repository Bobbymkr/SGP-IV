## PRODUCTION NOTES

### **Visual Style Guide**:
- **Color Scheme**: Professional blues and greens
- **Animation**: Smooth transitions, data visualizations
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif fonts
- **Pacing**: Dynamic but not rushed

### **Sound Design**:
- **Background Music**: Upbeat, professional, modern
- **Sound Effects**: Subtle traffic sounds, signal clicks
- **Voice Tone**: Confident, clear, professional

### **Technical Requirements**:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264)
- **Duration**: Exactly 3 minutes

### **Key Messages to Emphasize**:
1. **Problem**: Fixed timers are inefficient
2. **Solution**: AI-powered real-time adaptation
3. **Benefits**: Time savings, environmental impact, safety
4. **Future**: Smart city transformation

### **Target Audience Considerations**:
- **Non-technical**: Avoid jargon, use simple analogies
- **Decision makers**: Focus on ROI and benefits
- **General public**: Emphasize quality of life improvements
- **Technical stakeholders**: Highlight innovation and reliability
